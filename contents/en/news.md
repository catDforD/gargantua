### **National First Prize in China College Student Engineering Practice and Innovation Ability Competition**
**September 2025**

Our team (Wang Songlin et al.) won the **National First Prize** in the "Intelligent Logistics Handling" event at the 2025 China College Student Engineering Practice and Innovation Ability Competition. The competition had 3,268 universities and 86,746 participants.
